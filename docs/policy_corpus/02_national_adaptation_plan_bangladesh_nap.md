# National Adaptation Plan (NAP) of Bangladesh (2023–2050) — Operational Summary

**Publishing Authority:** Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC), Bangladesh  
**Target Ecological Zones:** 8 Distinct National Ecological Zones  
**Key Implementing Agencies:** BWDB, DAE, LGED, DoE, BMD, RAJUK  

---

## 1. Ecological Zone Interventions
The National Adaptation Plan categorizes national disaster risk into 8 operational zones:
1. **Haor and Wetland Basin (Sylhet Division):** Flash flood preparedness, submersible embankments, and village protection walls in Sunamganj, Netrokona, Habiganj, and Kishoreganj.
2. **Coastal Zone (Khulna, Barishal, Chattogram Divisions):** Salinity-tolerant crop buffers, tidal river management (TRM), and mangrove greenbelts across coastal polders.
3. **Barind and Drought-Prone Zone (Rajshahi & Rangpur Divisions):** Water retention ponds, agroforestry, and extreme heat action plans for construction workers and rickshaw pullers.
4. **Active River Floodplain & Charlands (Brahmaputra, Jamuna, Padma):** Raised homestead plinths, flood shelters, and community-managed emergency grain silos in Kurigram, Gaibandha, and Sirajganj.

## 2. Thresholds for Automated Disaster Response
- **River Discharge Alert Threshold:** When GloFAS or BWDB telemetry indicates river discharge exceeds the 75th percentile ($p75$) and forecasts 24-hour river stage above danger level, emergency sluice gate opening and embankment reinforcement must activate.
- **Urban Heat Threshold:** When daytime apparent temperature exceeds 40°C for two consecutive days, municipal authorities must open air-conditioned public cooling centers and distribute potable water along major transit hubs.

## 3. Policy Outcome Evaluation Protocol
- Every public adaptation scheme must record a pre-intervention baseline, an explicit target KPI (e.g. reduction in days of waterlogging, hectares of canopy planted), and be subjected to a 3-year post-completion independent audit.
