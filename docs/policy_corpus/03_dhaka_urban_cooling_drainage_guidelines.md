# Dhaka Urban Cooling, Heatwave Mitigation & Drainage Guidelines

**Publishing Authority:** Rajdhani Unnayan Kartripakkha (RAJUK), DNCC, and DSCC  
**Scope:** Dhaka Metropolitan Area (RAJUK Jurisdiction)  
**Applicability:** Urban Heat Island (UHI) Mitigation & Monsoon Drainage Management  

---

## 1. Urban Heat Island (UHI) Directives
Dhaka's dense impervious surfaces, concrete high-rises, and sparse tree canopy generate an urban heat island effect raising local temperatures by up to 3.5°C compared to surrounding peri-urban areas.

### Mandatory Interventions:
1. **Cool Roof Mandates:**
   - Commercial and high-rise residential rooftops must install high-albedo coatings (solar reflectance index SRI > 78) or rooftop gardens.
   - Subsidies provided for low-income informal settlements to apply reflective solar paint to corrugated tin roofs, reducing indoor ambient temperatures by 2.5°C to 4.0°C.
2. **Urban Micro-Canopy Planting:**
   - Priority planting along arterial transit corridors (Mirpur Road, Airport Road, Pragati Sarani) and pedestrian footpaths.
   - School zones and community health clinics must maintain at least 25% shaded perimeter cover.

## 2. Stormwater Drainage & Canal Revival Directives
- **Zero-Encroachment Buffer on Natural Khal:** Kallayanpur, Ramchandrapur, Rupnagar, and Mandail canals must retain a 15-meter natural riparian buffer free from unauthorized concrete structures.
- **Permeable Pavement Standard:** All newly paved public sidewalks and parking facilities must incorporate permeable interlocking concrete pavers to facilitate groundwater recharge and reduce street waterlogging.
- **Pumping Station Maintenance:** Kalyanpur and Rampura retention basins must maintain 100% operational readiness 48 hours prior to forecasted heavy monsoon downpours.
