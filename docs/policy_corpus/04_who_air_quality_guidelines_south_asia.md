# Ambient Air Quality Guidelines & South Asian Context (WHO / DoE Bangladesh)

**Publishing Authority:** World Health Organization (WHO) & Department of Environment (DoE), Bangladesh  
**Applicability:** Particulate Matter (PM2.5, PM10) Mitigation & Sensor Monitoring  

---

## 1. WHO Air Quality Guidelines vs. Bangladesh National Ambient Standards
Fine particulate matter ($PM_{2.5}$) poses severe respiratory and cardiovascular risks across dense South Asian metropolitan corridors.

| Parameter | WHO 2021 Guideline | WHO Interim Target-1 | Bangladesh National Standard (DoE) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **PM2.5 Annual Mean** | $5\,\mu\text{g}/\text{m}^3$ | $35\,\mu\text{g}/\text{m}^3$ | $15\,\mu\text{g}/\text{m}^3$ |
| **PM2.5 24-Hour Mean** | $15\,\mu\text{g}/\text{m}^3$ | $75\,\mu\text{g}/\text{m}^3$ | $65\,\mu\text{g}/\text{m}^3$ |
| **PM10 Annual Mean** | $15\,\mu\text{g}/\text{m}^3$ | $70\,\mu\text{g}/\text{m}^3$ | $50\,\mu\text{g}/\text{m}^3$ |
| **PM10 24-Hour Mean** | $45\,\mu\text{g}/\text{m}^3$ | $150\,\mu\text{g}/\text{m}^3$ | $150\,\mu\text{g}/\text{m}^3$ |

## 2. Sensor Scarcity & Monitoring Equity
- In Bangladesh, permanent reference-grade Continuous Ambient Air Quality Monitoring Stations (CAMS) operate in only 12 out of 64 districts (primarily Dhaka, Chattogram, Rajshahi, Khulna, Gazipur, Narayanganj).
- Over 50 districts lack official ground monitoring, creating an information blind spot where high rural pollution from biomass burning and traditional brick kilns goes unmeasured.
- **Policy Directive:** When allocating municipal air mitigation funds, lack of an official station must not be interpreted as absence of pollution. Municipal budgets must allocate capital for low-cost calibrated optical particle counters in unmonitored districts.

## 3. Targeted Air Quality Interventions
1. **Traditional Brick Kiln Modernization:** Enforce conversion from Fixed Chimney Kilns (FCK) to Zigzag or Tunnel kilns with particulate scrubbers.
2. **Dust Suppression in Construction Zones:** Mandatory wet-spraying twice daily on major infrastructure corridors during the dry season (November to March).
3. **Clean Public Transport Fleets:** Phasing out high-emission diesel transit vehicles with compressed natural gas (CNG) and electric buses.
