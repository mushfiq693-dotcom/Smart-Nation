# Mujib Climate Prosperity Plan (MCPP) Decade 2030 — Core Strategic Guidelines

**Publishing Authority:** Government of the People's Republic of Bangladesh / Climate Vulnerable Forum (CVF)  
**Reporting Horizon:** 2022–2030  
**Strategic Doctrine:** From Vulnerability to Resilience to Prosperity  

---

## 1. National Vulnerability & Adaptation Priorities
Bangladesh faces intersecting climate hazards spanning riverine monsoonal floods, storm-surge coastal inundation, and extreme urban heatwaves. The MCPP mandates that national and municipal development expenditure prioritize:
- **Coastal Delta Resilience:** Upgrading multi-purpose cyclone shelters and polder embankments across coastal districts (Satkhira, Khulna, Bagerhat, Barguna, Patuakhali, Bhola).
- **River Basin Flood Management:** Active morphologic sediment management and flood bypasses along the Brahmaputra-Jamuna and Meghna river corridors (Kurigram, Gaibandha, Sirajganj, Sunamganj).
- **Northwest Drought & Heat Adaptation:** Groundwater recharging, heatwave early warnings, and solar-powered micro-irrigation across the Barind Tract (Rajshahi, Chapai Nawabganj, Naogaon).

## 2. Resource Allocation & Equity Guardrails
Under Section 4.2 of the MCPP financing framework:
- Public climate adaptation finance must balance raw demographic exposure with an explicit vulnerability correction factor.
- Informal urban settlements, riverine char communities, and data-sparse peripheral upazilas must not be deprioritized simply because they lack permanent historical monitoring infrastructure.
- Budget allocation models must incorporate an equity weighting coefficient ($\alpha_{\text{equity}}$) to divert marginal resilience funds toward historically underserved districts.

## 3. Measurable 2030 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Reduce vulnerable population exposure to extreme heat and flooding by at least 30% by 2030.
- Expand municipal urban canopy cover to at least 20% across all metropolitan city corporations.
- Deploy localized multi-hazard early warning systems in 100% of hazard-prone upazilas.
- Mobilize up to \$80 Billion in public-private green bonds, municipal resilience grants, and international climate finance.
